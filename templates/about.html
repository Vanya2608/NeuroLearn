About Neuro_Learn

Our Mission

Reading can be challenging for children and adults with dyslexia. That's why we built Neuro_Learn—a platform specifically engineered to adjust how text is presented, so that reading becomes a joyful experience rather than a difficult struggle.

Key Features:

  • Customization: Dyslexia-friendly fonts, spacing adjustments, and color themes.
  • Simplification: Synonym replacement for complex English words.
  • Focus: Color-coded highlighting for visually similar and easily confused words.
  • Audio Support: Text-to-speech functionality to follow along aloud.
